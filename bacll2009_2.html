Part 5 — Writing

15 marks · 100–150 words

Write about "Traffic Accident Issues in Our Society."

Need a starting point?
  • Say how common traffic accidents are where you live.
  • Give two or three causes — speeding, drink driving, poor roads, not wearing helmets.
  • Explain the effects on families and the community.
  • Suggest what drivers, pedestrians, or the government could do differently.
