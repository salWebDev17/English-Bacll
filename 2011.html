Show a sample answer
Keeping our city clean is every citizen's duty, and small daily habits make the biggest difference. Firstly, everyone should throw rubbish only into bins, never onto the street or into rivers, since litter spoils both the view and public health. In addition, we should separate waste for recycling, especially plastic bottles and paper, so that fewer materials end up in landfill. Moreover, students and young people can join community clean-up days, sweeping streets and clearing drains before the rainy season causes flooding. We should also avoid single-use plastic bags when shopping, choosing cloth bags instead. Finally, we can raise awareness by sharing clean-city photos on social media and gently reminding tourists and neighbours to dispose of waste properly. If every household follows these simple habits, our city will truly deserve its reputation as a Kingdom of Wonder — clean, green, and welcoming to all who visit.
